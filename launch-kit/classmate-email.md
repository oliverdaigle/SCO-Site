# Email to classmates

**Subject:** A free tool for our ops homework (and a favor)

Hi everyone,

I built a site called Lotwise that solves the kinds of problems we do in class: EOQ, safety stock, newsvendor, forecast error, queuing, control charts, critical path, and more (93 calculators in all). Learner mode shows every step with the numbers plugged in, lets you pick your textbook's notation, and points out the usual exam traps. There are practice problems with worked solutions and a one-page formula sheet for each chapter.

All the calculators are free and you don't need an account: lotwise.io

The favor: if you check your homework against it and get a different answer, please send me the problem. Either I have a bug or there's a convention difference worth explaining, and I want to know about both.
