# TikTok / Reels script (about 40 seconds, vertical 9:16)

Record the screen on a phone in portrait, or record the desktop site and crop. Keep each on-screen line short enough to read in two seconds.

| Time | On screen | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 3 s | Close-up of the landing page headline. Text: "How much safety stock does a coffee shop need?" | "How much milk should a coffee shop keep on hand so it never runs out?" |
| 3 to 9 s | Type "I keep running out of stock" into the search bar. The safety stock calculator opens. | "I typed the problem in plain English and it picked the right formula." |
| 9 to 16 s | Change daily demand and lead time. The answer sentence and the chart update live. | "Change the numbers and the answer and the chart move with you. It even converts days and weeks for you." |
| 16 to 23 s | Drag the service level slider from 90% to 99%. Text: "Every extra 1% costs more." | "Going from 95 to 99 percent in stock costs way more buffer than you'd think. Watch it jump." |
| 23 to 31 s | Toggle to Learner mode. Scroll the step-by-step solution. Text: "Same math, every step shown." | "If you're a student, flip to Learner mode and it shows every step in your textbook's notation." |
| 31 to 37 s | Verified page. Text: "2.3 million values checked against independent code." | "And every formula is tested against separately written code and simulations, so you can trust it." |
| 37 to 40 s | Logo and URL. Text: "93 calculators. Free. lotwise.io" | "93 calculators, all free. Link in bio." |

**Caption:** 93 free supply chain calculators. Plain answers for work, full steps for class. #supplychain #operationsmanagement

**Notes:** Film at the highest brightness, hide notifications, and use the dark theme for contrast. The demo video in this folder has usable clips for every beat.
