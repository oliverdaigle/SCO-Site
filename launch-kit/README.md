# Lotwise launch kit

Everything here is written in your voice and ready to post. None of it uses em dashes. Replace `lotwise.io` everywhere if you launch on a different domain.

| File | What it is | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| `reddit-supplychain.md` | Launch post for practitioners | r/supplychain |
| `reddit-operations.md` | Launch post with an operations angle | r/operations |
| `linkedin.md` | Personal launch post | LinkedIn. Add one or two hashtags at the end if you like them. |
| `tiktok-reels-script.md` | 40-second shot list with voiceover | TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts |
| `classmate-email.md` | Short email asking classmates to try it and report differences | Course email list or group chat |
| `demo-video.mp4` | 56-second screen capture of real use, with captions | Product Hunt, LinkedIn, the landing page, Reddit comments |
| `screenshots/` | Five polished screenshots at 2x resolution | Product Hunt gallery, social cards, App directories |

## Order that tends to work

1. Send the classmate email first. Friendly users find bugs before strangers do.
2. Post to r/supplychain and r/operations a few days later, on a weekday morning (US time). Read each subreddit's self-promotion rules first; both posts disclose the paid tier.
3. Post on LinkedIn the same week, and attach the demo video natively rather than linking to it.
4. Film the TikTok or Reel last, once you know which part of the demo people ask about.

## Replying to comments

When someone reports a result that disagrees with their textbook or their company's method, thank them, find out which convention they use, and reply with the setting that matches it (for example the table z option, or holding cost as a percentage). If it is a real bug, say so plainly and fix it. The Verified page is your best answer to "why should I trust this?"
